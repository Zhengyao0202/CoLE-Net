CoLE-Net

Code↗

Clinical sampling and local MRI modeling, linked by recorded biopsy evidence.

Overview

Clinical labels originate from sampled tissue. The Consistent Local Evidence Network (CoLE-Net) makes regional categorical evidence explicit and connects it to the patient prediction through spatial context and tumor geometry.

The study includes 1,997 patients across six cohorts and evaluates IDH mutation, 1p/19q codeletion and histologic grade. A 15-patient cohort with recorded biopsy coordinates provides regional validation. This links patient-level discrimination with the diagnostic meaning and consistency of regional predictions.

Framework

Regional likelihoods support both patient-level prediction and assessment at a recorded biopsy site.

Patient-level results

CoLE-Net is compared with 19 baseline models across three prediction tasks and three evaluation cohorts. The six conventional models share 30 T1 intensity and tumor shape/location features.

Complete results and statistical comparisons

Patient-level and biopsy comparisons use the same fitted conventional models. The six conventional configurations were selected after inspecting outcomes, with 95% weight on biopsy performance and 5% on patient-level performance. These comparisons are exploratory; the downloads retain the full 19-baseline panel and search-adjusted sensitivity results.
